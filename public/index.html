How it works

Four steps, no mystery.

  1. Bring it in

    Drop by with the garment, or book a slot if you would rather have the bench to yourself.

  2. Get pinned

    We fit it on you, pin it, and talk through what is possible and what is not. Estimates are given before anything is cut.

  3. We work

    Most alterations take three days. Complex reconstruction takes two weeks. You will hear from us either way.

  4. Wear it out

    Final fitting in the shop. If anything still pulls, it goes back on the bench at no charge.

The atelier

A small room and a very good iron.

Deneuve started as one machine in a back room and has stayed deliberately small. Two cutters, one presser, and a rule that nothing leaves the shop unless it would be worn by the person who made it.

  • Deadstock first. Cloth is sourced from mill ends and closed runs before anything new is ordered.
  • Hand-finished. Buttonholes, linings and hems are worked by hand, because they last longer that way.
  • Repaired forever. Anything bought here comes back for free mending, for as long as you own it.
